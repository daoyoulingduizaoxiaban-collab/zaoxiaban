## QA Smoke Policy

From the next retest onward, MVP GUI gate should be judged by real user workflows, not by requiring all 27 routes to pass direct `reLaunch`.

| Route Type | Expected QA Entry |
|---|---|
| Tab pages | Use `switchTab` and verify tab state/content |
| Detail pages | Enter from the owning list/card with required id/query |
| Picker/eventChannel pages | Enter from the workflow that opens the picker |
| Customer share/order entry | Enter with `groupOrderId` or real share path |
| Standalone utility pages | Direct open is acceptable if the app supports it |

Direct 27-route smoke can still be recorded, but a direct-open timeout should be treated as a suspected route-entry/guard issue, not automatically as a failed real-user workflow.
